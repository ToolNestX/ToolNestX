About ToolNestX

ToolNestX is a free all-in-one online toolbox offering more than a dozen handy utilities — from word counters and password generators to unit converters and QR code generators.

Our goal is simple: give everyone fast, easy-to-use tools without needing to sign up, install anything, or pay a single rupee.

All tools run directly in your browser, meaning your data stays on your device — nothing is uploaded to any server.

We're constantly working on adding new tools to make ToolNestX the only toolbox you'll ever need.

Thank you for using ToolNestX!
